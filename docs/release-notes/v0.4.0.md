Print, Page Setup, and Export to PDF, for files of any size.

## Printing and PDF

- **Print.** **File ▸ Print…** (**Ctrl/⌘ Alt P**) lays out the pages and opens **Print Preview** with every page, then the system print dialog. A status-bar progress bar and **Cancel** show while the pages are prepared.
- **Export to PDF.** **File ▸ Export to PDF…** writes a PDF in the background, with progress and **Cancel**, and works on files of any size, because pages are written to disk as they are laid out. Text in the PDF can be selected, searched, and copied, and long PDFs get an outline with one entry per 100 pages.
- **Printed pages and the PDF match** line for line and page for page, because Nulis lays out the pages once for both.
- **Page Setup.** **File ▸ Page Setup…** sets the paper (A4, Letter, Legal, A3, A5) and orientation, margins, font size, whether long lines wrap or are cut, colors (print colors, black and white, or the current theme), line numbers, syntax highlighting, and a header and footer with the file name, path, date, and page numbers. A preview of the first page follows every change.
- **Selections and hex dumps.** **Export Selection to PDF…** exports each selection with its own line numbers. In the hexadecimal editor, Print and Export to PDF produce a hex dump.
- **Markdown** in View mode prints as the formatted preview.
- **Other scripts.** Chinese, Japanese, and Korean characters print from the fonts installed on your computer and keep columns lined up.
- Above 1,000 pages Nulis asks before going on. Print stops at 5,000 pages; Export to PDF has no limit.

The user guide and Nulis Help have a new section, *Printing and PDF*.

## Removed

- **Remote files (SFTP) were removed**, with the Remote panel. To edit files on a server, mount it as a network drive or copy the files over. Nulis no longer stores passwords in the system keychain, and the application is smaller without the SSH and OpenSSL code.

## First launch

These builds are not signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate, so each system asks once.

- **macOS:** open the `.dmg` and drag Nulis into Applications, then open it. When macOS says it could not verify Nulis, click **Done**. Open **System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security**, scroll to **Security**, and click **Open Anyway** next to the message about Nulis; confirm with your password or Touch ID. You do this once per installed version.
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen shows *Windows protected your PC*, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** no extra step; the package manager installs the libraries Nulis needs.

## Known limitations

- Scripts whose letters join or reorder, such as Arabic and Devanagari, print one letter at a time and do not look right. Emoji are left blank in PDFs, and Nulis says how many characters it could not print.
- A column selection exports the whole lines it spans.
- Printing was checked by hand on macOS 15, Windows 11, and Ubuntu 26.04. On Windows, check the paper in the system print dialog: Page Setup sets it on macOS and Linux only.
- Builds are not signed with a Developer ID or code-signing certificate, hence the first-launch steps above.
- On Windows and Linux, Nulis does not yet add itself to the system's "Open with" list.
- Linux packages are for 64-bit x86 only.
