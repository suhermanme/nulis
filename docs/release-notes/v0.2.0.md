## What's new

- **A new look.** A new app icon: an N whose last stroke is the text cursor. The About window now has a batik background, dark or light to match your theme.
- **Find and Replace window.** Find, Replace, Find in Files, and Replace in Files in one window you can move anywhere. Search the document, the selection, or all open files; list every matching line and jump to one; bookmark them all. Replace in Files shows a preview and asks before changing files on disk.
- **Files changed elsewhere.** When another program changes or deletes an open file, Nulis asks whether to reload it or keep your version. **File ▸ Reload from Disk** reloads on demand.
- **Quick Open and recent files.** **Ctrl/⌘ P** finds a file by name among open files, the opened folder, and recent files. **File ▸ Open Recent…** lists the last 20 files.
- **File commands.** Save All, Close All Files, Rename File (unsaved changes are kept), and Show in Places.
- **Editing.** Smart Home and End, forward Delete, Select All and Select Line, Duplicate, Move, and Delete Line, and a right-click menu in the editor.
- **Format menu.** Change case, indent and outdent (Tab and Shift Tab), toggle line comments, trim trailing spaces, convert tabs and spaces, sort lines, Base64, and insert the date and time.
- **Search and navigation.** F3 and Shift F3 repeat a search; go to the matching bracket, to a line and column, back and forward through jumps, and to the next or previous change; list bookmarks. The status bar shows word and line counts and the code point at the caret.
- **Line endings.** The status bar shows whether a file uses LF, CRLF, CR, or a mix; click it to convert. Enter keeps the line ending of the line you are on.
- **View options.** Word wrap (**Alt Z**, remembered), zoom (**Ctrl/⌘ = - 0**), show whitespace and line endings, hide line numbers, a column marker at 80, and Always on Top.
- **Column and clipboard tools.** Insert a number sequence down a column, add up selected numbers, justify text in a column block, and line up comma- or tab-separated columns. Copy or cut and append to the clipboard, swap the selection with the clipboard, and paste from a history of this session's copies.

## Fixes

- **Windows:** the Settings, Find and Replace, and Help windows no longer open blank or show the application menu, and Find reopens instantly.
- The document map shows whole lines and fits long files, and a click goes to the matching line.

## First launch

These builds are not signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate, so each system asks once.

- **macOS:** open the `.dmg` and drag Nulis into Applications, then open it. When macOS says it could not verify Nulis, click **Done**. Open **System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security**, scroll to **Security**, and click **Open Anyway** next to the message about Nulis; confirm with your password or Touch ID. You do this once per installed version. If the Dock still shows the old icon after updating, restart the Dock or log out and back in.
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen shows *Windows protected your PC*, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** no extra step; the package manager installs the libraries Nulis needs.

## Known limitations

- Builds are not signed with a Developer ID or code-signing certificate, hence the first-launch steps above.
- On Windows and Linux, Nulis does not yet add itself to the system's "Open with" list.
- Linux packages are for 64-bit x86 only.
